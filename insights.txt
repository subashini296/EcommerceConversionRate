E-COMMERCE CONVERSION RATE - KEY INSIGHTS
=========================================
Period: 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31  |  Records: 5,475

HEADLINE NUMBERS
- Total visitors      : 1,644,057
- Product views       : 979,159  (59.6% of visitors)
- Add to cart         : 196,964  (20.1% of views)
- Purchases           : 69,877  (35.5% of carts)
- Overall conversion  : 4.25%  (purchases / visitors)
- Revenue             : Rs. 185,248,721   |  Avg order value: Rs. 2,651

FUNNEL
1. Biggest drop-off: Product View -> Add to Cart (79.9% of users lost at this step).
2. Cart abandonment rate: 64.5% of carts never reach purchase.

TIME PATTERNS
3. Best month: November (5.21%); weakest month: February (3.82%).
4. Day of week has almost no effect (gap only 0.18 pts between Friday and Tuesday); weekday vs weekend is not a lever.

SEGMENTS
5. Best channel: Email (9.21%); weakest: Social Media (1.73%).
6. Highest revenue channel: Organic Search (Rs. 56,258,946).
7. Best device: Desktop (5.10%); weakest: Mobile (3.80%).
   Mobile brings 62% of visitors.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Reduce cart abandonment: cart reminder emails, guest checkout, clear shipping cost early.
- Improve the mobile experience (faster pages, simpler checkout), as it converts lowest.
- Shift budget toward high-converting channels (Email); fix landing pages for Social Media.
- Plan campaigns and stock around peak months (November) and keep offers running in slow months (February).
- Run A/B tests on the Product View -> Add to Cart step, since it loses the most users.
